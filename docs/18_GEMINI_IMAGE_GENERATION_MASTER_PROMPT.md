# Gemini Master Prompt — Pizza Wave Prototype Image Generation

You are creating the complete visual asset pack for a premium, youthful, mobile-first QSR ordering PWA called **The Pizza Wave**, based in Puri, Odisha, India.

This is NOT a generic pizza brand and NOT a luxury restaurant.

The visual identity is:
- playful
- contemporary
- editorial
- youthful
- real-food focused
- bold but clean
- Indian-local without becoming traditional or cliché
- suitable for a modern direct-ordering PWA

## BRAND COLORS

Use these backgrounds when requested:

- Tangerine Orange: `#FD7E3B`
- Rich Burgundy: `#4D0711`
- Sunshine Yellow: `#FDCC3B`
- Sky Blue: `#8BDFFA`
- Blossom Pink: `#FAAEDC`
- Leaf Green: `#C1CC52`
- Tomato Red: `#BD1F17`
- Soft Cream: `#FEFEF6`

IMPORTANT:
Do not put any written UI text into the generated images.
The application will add all typography separately.

## GLOBAL PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

All food must look:
- freshly prepared,
- believable,
- appetizing,
- slightly editorial,
- naturally textured,
- premium but still affordable QSR food,
- not fine-dining,
- not hyper-glossy,
- not synthetic-looking AI food.

Use:
- soft directional studio lighting,
- warm appetizing highlights,
- realistic shadows,
- crisp food texture,
- controlled depth of field,
- consistent color grading across the full image set.

Avoid:
- fake ingredients,
- impossible cheese,
- deformed food,
- excessive garnish,
- cutlery unless naturally needed,
- giant logos,
- text,
- watermarks,
- branded third-party packaging,
- generic Western fast-food styling.

The same product should look visually consistent across every image.

## HUMAN REPRESENTATION

Lifestyle images should naturally feature contemporary Indian customers.

Use:
- university-age students,
- young professionals,
- families,
- friend groups,
- couples where required.

People should look candid and natural.

Avoid:
- everyone looking at camera,
- staged corporate poses,
- luxury fashion-shoot styling,
- stereotypical cultural costumes,
- generic foreign stock-photo feeling.

Food must remain visually important.

## PRODUCT CAMERA SYSTEM

For all isolated product images:
- 1:1 square master
- three-quarter front angle
- slightly elevated viewpoint
- consistent camera height
- same soft key light from upper-left/front-left
- gentle realistic shadow
- clean visual separation
- centered enough for product card cropping
- leave 8–12% breathing room around food
- preferably isolated on a clean neutral/transparent-looking studio surface so background removal is easy

Do NOT add text, labels, price cards, plates with logos, or props that prevent clean cutout usage.

# CONSISTENCY CHECK

Before finalizing the set, verify:

1. all pizzas look like they belong to the same restaurant,
2. lighting is consistent across product cutouts,
3. humans look naturally Indian and contemporary,
4. no image contains generated typography,
5. no third-party logos are visible,
6. no product looks unrealistically luxurious compared with a ₹100–₹500 QSR menu,
7. all images have enough crop flexibility for mobile cards,
8. the palette complements Burgundy `#4D0711` and Cream `#FEFEF6`,
9. the overall look is bold/editorial but still suitable for an ordering app,
10. each output is generated as a separate asset.
